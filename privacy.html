Privacy Policy

How Mokie protects information when you use AI styling, outfit generation, beauty inspiration, community challenges, and fashion chat features.

Effective date: July 6, 2026

Our AI privacy commitment. Mokie's core AI models are built, trained, and operated by our own team, without third-party AI model providers. We do not collect personal information for sale, rent, advertising resale, unrelated profiling, or unnecessary sharing. User privacy is protected through minimized processing and limited purpose use.

1. Information You Choose to Provide

Mokie may process content you choose to enter or upload, such as profile display details, outfit photos, beauty preferences, style prompts, chat messages, saved looks, challenge entries, and support emails. You control whether to provide optional content. If you upload an image of a person, you are responsible for having permission to use it.

2. App Operation Data

To keep Mokie functional, we may process limited technical data such as app version, device type, language, approximate region, timestamps, feature status, generation progress, crash data, and error logs. This information is used to operate the app, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. We do not use it to sell user profiles.

3. AI Processing

When you use Mokie's AI style assistant or outfit generator, the app processes the selected image, prompt, color preference, occasion, mood, or styling option needed to create the requested output. This processing is limited to delivering the feature, showing the result, improving safety, troubleshooting failures, and maintaining service security.

We do not use your uploads for facial recognition, biometric identification, identity verification, or advertising targeting. User-uploaded images are not treated as public training data and are not sold, rented, traded, or shared with third parties for their independent use.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide AI outfit ideas, makeup inspiration, color guidance, saved looks, and chat-style styling responses.
  • Operate community challenge pages and display content you intentionally publish.
  • Maintain account continuity, app settings, support communication, and service reliability.
  • Detect misuse, spam, unsafe prompts, technical failures, or unauthorized access attempts.
  • Understand aggregated product performance without exposing private user content.

5. Sharing

We do not sell user information. We do not share personal information for third-party advertising networks or data brokers. Limited disclosure may occur only when necessary to operate infrastructure under our instructions, complete Apple in-app purchase processes, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, respond to support, or complete a business transfer subject to privacy protections.

6. Public Features

If you post to a challenge, community feed, or public style area, that content may be visible to other users. Public participation is optional. Do not publish photos, messages, or generated looks that you want to keep private.

7. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide Mokie, maintain your experience, comply with law, resolve disputes, protect safety, and enforce terms. AI inputs and outputs may be stored for delivery, history, troubleshooting, and safety for a limited period unless deleted or unless longer retention is legally required.

8. Your Choices

You may avoid optional uploads, decline optional device permissions, delete available saved content, edit profile details where supported, or contact us to request privacy help. Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, deletion, or limitation of certain information. Some requests may be limited by security, legal, or technical requirements.

9. Children

Mokie is not intended for children below the minimum digital consent age in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and misuse. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so please use care when uploading photos or publishing community content.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@mokie.fun. We may update this Policy as Mokie changes, and the latest version will show the effective date above.
