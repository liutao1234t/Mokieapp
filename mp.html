Mokie Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026 · Contact: support@mokie.fun

This Privacy Policy explains how Mokie collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access the Service.

Mokie is an entertainment-oriented movie dubbing and voice participation platform. Some features may involve AI-assisted audio processing, creator participation systems, or short-form media interaction.

1. Information We Collect

  • account registration information including username, email address, and profile information;
  • voice recordings, dubbed audio, comments, creator submissions, uploaded clips, and interaction content;
  • support requests, moderation reports, appeals, and feedback;
  • technical information including device model, operating system, IP address, app version, and language settings;
  • interaction history including viewed clips, dubbing activity, creator engagement, comments, likes, follows, and search activity.

2. How We Use Information

  • operate and maintain the Service;
  • provide dubbing and entertainment features;
  • deliver creator and community systems;
  • provide AI-assisted entertainment tools;
  • improve recommendation systems and Service quality;
  • maintain safety, moderation, fraud prevention, and platform integrity;
  • respond to support requests and legal obligations.

3. Movie Clip Source and Intellectual Property Statement

Mokie respects copyright and intellectual property rights.

Movie-related clips appearing within the Service are generally limited to publicly available trailers, teaser content, promotional fragments, parody-oriented references, or user-uploaded content that users represent they are authorized to share.

Short-form media segments are generally maintained within limited durations and are intended for entertainment participation, dubbing interaction, parody, commentary, or fan creativity purposes.

Mokie does not encourage unauthorized distribution of complete copyrighted works and may respond to valid copyright notices or takedown requests.

4. AI-Assisted Features

Mokie may provide AI-assisted audio enhancement, entertainment recommendations, dubbing interaction systems, or voice-related AI features.

AI-generated outputs may contain fictional, inaccurate, incomplete, or entertainment-oriented information.

Users remain responsible for evaluating AI-generated content before relying on it.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers assisting with hosting, infrastructure, analytics, moderation, security, storage, customer support, or legal compliance.

Mokie does not publicly sell personal information in the ordinary sense.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, moderation, legal, fraud prevention, security, and platform integrity purposes.

7. User Rights

  • Users may update profile information within the Service;
  • users may request account deletion through support channels;
  • users may revoke device permissions through device settings;
  • users may opt out of certain promotional communications where applicable.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.
